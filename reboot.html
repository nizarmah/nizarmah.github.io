Reboot

Sunday, September 13, 2026


I learned to build stuff. And I lost the ability to share. I'm on a quest to get it back.

How did I get here? Well, I started out as an impostor.

As a kid, I stumbled on and joined the MCPE forum. I used to build mods by taking snippets from others.

YouTube was my way to prove I'm "legit." I shared everything I did there as well.

Somehow in a year or so, I blew up to 2.9K subs and 713K views.

A confident impostor, I had a new idea. We all shared a single laptop at home. To code whenever, I made a JS IDE for my Nexus 7. It blew up to 20,000 downloads.

How?! I didn't know how to code. I didn't want to use Rhino, a Java JS Engine. So the IDE was a WebView with JS files and console output.

After a lot of Ctrl+C Ctrl+V, it started to kick in. I started understanding what I'm doing.

I started writing about how I built something. It got me an audience and was taught in a university course.

I started learning more and writing less. I erased my social media presence and disappeared.

I tried to come back. I just never held my breath long enough.

This time it will be different...
